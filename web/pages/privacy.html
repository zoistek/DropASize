Privacy policy

DropASize · Last updated: 7 November 2026

DropASize is a wellness app for healthy eating, movement, fasting and habits, with support from a coach. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

Who we are: Zoistek Ltd, 128 City Road, London, EC1V 2NX, United Kingdom. Contact: info@zoistek.com.

Who can see your data

  • You.
  • Your coach (the DropASize coaching team) can always see your name, goal and plan tier, and the targets and training plan they set for you. Only if you turn on sharing with your coach can they also see your why, check-ins (mood and weight), streak, cravings, wins, reflections and weekly insights. You can turn sharing off at any time. Your food diary, cycle data and progress photos stay private and are never shown to your coach. Messages your coach sends you are stored so you can read them.
  • Other members can see your display name, the first letter of your name, and your 7-day points, streak and workout count on the leaderboard. If you join the Squad, members of your squad can also see your daily steps and habit ticks.

Service providers

  • Supabase hosts our database, logins and file storage (EU region).
  • OpenAI processes AI requests. When you use an AI feature, we send your message, any photo you choose and the profile details needed (such as your first name, goals and diet), but not your email address. OpenAI processes this to provide the answer under its API terms.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts supplies the app's typeface, so your device requests font files from Google.

AI features

AI answers can be wrong. They are general information, not medical advice. You can edit nutrition estimates, and report any AI answer from inside the app. Reports are reviewed by our team.

Health Connect (Android app)

If you choose to connect Health Connect, the Android app reads your daily step counts only (today and the previous 7 days), each time you open the app, return to it, or tap “Sync now”. We ask only for permission to read steps. We never read any other health data and never write to Health Connect.

Why: to fill in your daily steps automatically, so your steps ring, step goals, Squad and challenge progress, leaderboard points and coach summary are accurate without typing them in. If you also add steps by hand for the same day, we count the higher of the two numbers, never both.

Imported step counts are saved with your account like steps you enter yourself, and are shown to your coach and, if you join the Squad, to your squad. Data from Health Connect is used only to provide these features. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train AI models. Our use of information received from Health Connect follows the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How to disconnect: in the app, tap your photo (top right) → Health Connect → Disconnect. This stops syncing and removes the app’s access in Health Connect. You can also remove access at any time in Android Settings → Health Connect → App permissions → DropASize. Steps already imported stay in your account; you can delete them by deleting your account.

Security and retention

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and protected by access rules so members can only read their own data. We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and associated data are deleted from our systems. Backups held by our hosting provider are removed in line with their backup cycle. Our server logs record the deleted account’s ID and are kept for the limited period set by our hosting provider. Reports other members made about you (which include your account ID and display name) are kept so our team can complete moderation.

Deleting your account

In the app: tap your photo (top right) → Delete account. Or on the web: delete your account. This removes your login, profile, saved data, photos, messages, leaderboard entry and reports.

Your rights

You can access, download (in the app: Email, password and data → Download my data), correct or delete your data, and you can complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

Children

DropASize is for adults aged 18 and over.

Health disclaimer

DropASize is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Speak to your GP before big changes to diet, exercise, fasting or medication.
